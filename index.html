About Luthada

Luthada Mavhungu Mashudu, born on 18 May 2007, is a South African politician, entrepreneur, creative and public figure. He is a member of the Economic Liberators Forum South Africa (ELF-SA), having joined the organisation in November 2023.


He is the founder of African Child Records and Black Laurel Holdings, and is one half of DJ BIGGYSMALLS, officially becoming part of the duo in July 2024.


His work spans politics, entrepreneurship, youth-focused initiatives, business and music, with a focus on building platforms and opportunities for the next generation.
